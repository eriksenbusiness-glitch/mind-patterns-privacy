Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MIND / PATTERNS uses information when operating its social media publishing tools.

Information We Use

The publishing tool may use Instagram account identifiers, basic professional account information, and authorization tokens needed to connect to Instagram and publish content on the account owner's behalf.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authorize the connection to Instagram, prepare and publish content, and maintain the operation and security of the publishing tool. The tool is intended for managing content for MIND / PATTERNS.

Storage and Security

Authorization tokens and configuration details may be stored locally on the operator's computer for publishing. Reasonable measures are taken to protect this information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

MIND / PATTERNS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Meta and Instagram as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own policies and terms.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed to operate and secure the publishing tool. To request deletion of information held by MIND / PATTERNS, contact us using the email address below. Some information may remain with Meta in accordance with Meta's own policies.

Third-Party Services

Use of Instagram and other Meta services is also subject to Meta's applicable privacy policy and terms.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: mindpatternsinsta@outlook.com
